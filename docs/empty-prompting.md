# The Empty Prompting Problem

## The Problem

You open an AI tool, type "give me a product strategy for X," read the output, copy the best parts, close the tab. You did something. You have a document. But three months later, when you need to do this again, you're exactly where you started. You have an output, not a capability.

This is empty prompting: using AI as a content dispenser rather than a system for building judgment. The output looks professional. It has structure, coherent paragraphs, reasonable recommendations. But it contains nothing that could only come from you. Another person with the same tool and the same prompt would get something nearly identical.

The problem isn't the output — it's that producing the output didn't make you better at producing the next one.

---

## The Distinction That Matters

There's a precise difference between consuming AI and composing with AI:

| | Consuming AI | Composing with AI |
|---|---|---|
| Context | Prompt with no personal knowledge | Inject your vault, cases, frameworks |
| Output quality | Competent and generic | Specific to your judgment |
| Learning | None — the output leaves, your capability stays flat | Gaps surface → feed back into your system |
| Next time | Same starting point | Stronger vault → better output |
| Compound | Outputs accumulate | Capability compounds |

The analog in other domains is sharp. Someone who watches tennis videos on YouTube is consuming content about tennis. Someone who plays with a coach is practicing tennis. Both spend time. Only one gets better. Empty prompting is watching the videos.

---

## Why It Happens

**The default workflow skips context.** The path of least resistance is prompt → output. There's no step in the default workflow that asks "what do I already know about this?" Most people never install that step.

**The output looks good enough.** Because AI output is fluent and well-structured, it provides the feeling of having produced something valuable. The fact that it's indistinguishable from what anyone else would produce isn't visible until you compare — and most people never compare.

**There's no feedback loop.** The output disappears into a document, a Slack message, a presentation. It never returns to generate a signal about what's missing or what could be better. Every session starts from scratch.

**The shortcut doesn't announce itself.** In every technological revolution, a shortcut emerges that produces the appearance of value without building anything durable. The calculator without understanding the math. Wikipedia without reading the book. The pattern repeats. Empty prompting is this generation's version — and like previous shortcuts, it looks like productivity while it's happening.

---

## How to Detect It

Ask yourself these questions about your last AI-assisted output:

- **Could anyone else with the same tool have produced this?** If yes, your context wasn't present.
- **Does this output contain positions or examples that could only come from your specific experience?** If no, you consumed.
- **Did producing this output tell you anything about what you don't know?** If you can't answer, there was no diagnostic loop.
- **Is your ability to produce this type of output better now than six months ago?** If you genuinely can't tell, nothing has compounded.

The most direct test: take an output you produced "with AI help" and strip your name from it. Hand it to someone who knows your domain. Ask: "Does this sound like it came from someone with years of specific experience in this field?" If the answer is "it could have come from anyone," the prompting was empty.

---

## How to Fix It

**Install a pre-production step.** Before any AI-assisted task, run a retrieval step: What do I already know about this? What cases have I seen? What positions have I formed? Retrieve those notes first. Then prompt with that context included. This one structural change transforms empty prompting into composing.

**Use Phase 1 diagnostically, not as the destination.** The no-context output isn't a deliverable — it's a baseline. Produce it, then compare it to what you get with your vault context injected. The difference is the measure of how much your knowledge is actually contributing. If the difference is small, the vault needs work in this domain. If it's large, you're composing.

**Close the loop on gaps.** After every production session, capture what was missing. Where did the AI critique something you couldn't defend? Where did the output feel generic despite your context? Those gaps are learning signals — structured notes about what you need to know better. Without capturing them, the session ends and nothing compounds.

**Measure capability over time, not output count.** The indicator that you've stopped empty prompting is that your Phase 2 output on a topic is qualitatively different than it was six months ago — and you can point to specific vault content that explains why.
